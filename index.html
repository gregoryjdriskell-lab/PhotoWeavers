Photo guide

How to pick a photo that weaves beautifully

Weaving turns pictures into thread, so clear photos with strong contrast look best. A few quick rules make a big difference.

✓ Do this

  • Use good light. Daylight or a window beats flash and dim rooms.
  • Get close. Faces and subjects should fill most of the frame.
  • Keep the background simple. One clear subject reads better than a crowd.
  • Use the original file. Pick the highest-quality version, ideally at least [[MIN_RESOLUTION]].
  • Look for contrast. Clear difference between subject and background.

✕ Skip this

  • Blurry or tiny photos. Screenshots and heavily zoomed images lose detail.
  • Dark or backlit shots. Faces turn into shadows.
  • Busy backgrounds. Details get lost in the clutter.
  • Heavy filters. Start from the unedited original.
  • Tiny faces in big group shots. Crop in on the people who matter.
